Casal de Colònies — booking management for a summer camp

Built for a camp in Colònia de Sant Pere, Mallorca. In production.

The problem

A small camp managed its school group bookings for each season by hand. Agreements, annexes, deadlines and occupancy were spread over documents and email.

The solution

A portable desktop app that runs from a USB stick on any Windows machine, with no server and no installation. It manages schools and bookings and tracks the status of each booking: agreement sent, waiting for Annex 1, confirmed. It generates the booking agreement and Annex 1 as PDFs from customisable templates and emails them through the client's Gmail account. It also shows an occupancy calendar and upcoming deadlines, and includes a first-run checklist and a built-in manual.

The technical challenge

The app had to be portable and work offline. Electron packages a Next.js app, and the data lives in a local SQLite database through sql.js. Authentication is dual: Google sign-in when online and “continue offline” when not. Google OAuth had to work from a desktop app, and a documented migration path covers legacy data.

Delivered to a real client

This is not a demo. It was built for a real client and is in production, from requirements through delivery and support.
